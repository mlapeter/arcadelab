🎸Pitch Lab

🎸 Pull any string with your finger and let go to make it sing!

📏 A shorter string wobbles faster and sounds higher.

💪 More tension (a tighter string) also makes it higher.

🔊 Pull harder and the string plays louder.

🎵 Tap a string to pick it, then use the sliders to tune it. The little wave at the top shows the sound you hear!
